# Hi, I'm Niklas 👋

I build open-source projects for Home Assistant that make useful information easier to see, understand, and act on. My work ranges from Formula 1 race weekends to Swedish weather, public-safety alerts, traffic and focused e-ink displays.

## Featured projects

- 🏎️ **[F1 Sensor](https://github.com/Nicxe/f1_sensor)** — Formula 1 schedules, live timing, race updates and results for Home Assistant.
- 🌦️ **[SMHI Alerts](https://github.com/Nicxe/home-assistant-smhialerts)** — Swedish weather warnings and alerts for automations.
- 📢 **[Krisinformation](https://github.com/Nicxe/krisinformation)** — Swedish public-safety alerts for your dashboard and automations.
- 🚗 **[Trafikinfo SE](https://github.com/Nicxe/homeassistant-trafikinfo-se)** — Road traffic events from Trafikverket.
- 📟 **[ESPHome E-ink Display](https://github.com/Nicxe/esphome)** — focused, low-power information displays powered by ESPHome and Home Assistant.

## Open source, with community support

I maintain these projects in my spare time and aim to keep them useful, reliable, and free for everyone. A star helps other Home Assistant users find them, and an issue, translation, idea, or contribution helps make them better.

If one of my projects makes your day a little easier or a race weekend a little more fun, you can support the work with a [one-time or monthly GitHub sponsorship](https://github.com/sponsors/Nicxe). Thank you for being here. 💛
